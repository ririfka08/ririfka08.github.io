Case studies in Data Analysis

Revenue Loss Analysis

Cleaned and analyzed 10,586 rows across 5 relational tables in Google Sheets, then built an interactive Looker Studio dashboard to track revenue loss, cancellations, returns, and trends by category, brand, and region.

  • Revenue loss reached 15.34% (Rp235.01M of Rp1.53B potential revenue), driven mainly by cancellations (10.9%) rather than returns (5%)
  • Jacket was the top loss-driving category, while Cendana Co had the highest cancellation/return rate, highlighting areas for further quality and supplier investigation

Books Publishing Strategy Analysis

Analysis of 52,000+ books from Goodreads to recommend genre and catalog strategy for publishers — popular genres, the effect of page length, rating trends, and underserved language opportunities.

  • Niche genres (Comic Strips, Church, Manga) actually outrate mainstream genres like general Fiction
  • Identified 4 underserved language markets: Greek Modern, Filipino, Japanese, Bulgarian

Netflix Content Strategy Analysis

Multi-table analysis (joining titles & credits datasets) for streaming platform content strategy — underserved genres, Movie vs TV Show comparison, production-country efficiency, and cast-level analysis.

  • TV Shows outperform Movies — average IMDb rating of 6.98 vs 6.25
